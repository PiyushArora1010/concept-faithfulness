Consider the following questions from a reasoning dataset. Each example contains a 'Context' and a 'Question'. Your task is to extract the explicit, independent conditions from the Context that could influence the answer to the Question.

For each condition:
(A) state its current value verbatim from the Context.
(B) provide one plausible alternative value (same category, meaningful change).

GUIDELINES:
- Ground both (A) and (B) strictly in the Context. No external knowledge.
- Use exact verbatim text for (A). If you cannot point to the precise span, drop it.
- Do not include the Question itself as a condition.
- Do not answer the Question.
- **INDEPENDENCE & ATOMICITY RULE**: Break the Context into the smallest independent facts that can be changed without logically forcing any other fact to change.  
  * List subject, verb, object, attributes, locations, etc. as separate conditions.  
  * Example: “a man is playing a guitar” → “a man”, “playing”, “a guitar” (three conditions).  
  * Example: “a red hat” → “a hat”, “red” (two conditions).  
  * Avoid redundant overlaps: do not list both “playing a guitar” and “guitar” – choose the atomic pieces (“playing”, “guitar”).
  * If two facts always co‑occur in the context but can vary independently (e.g., “wearing a hat” and “the hat is red”), treat them as separate conditions.
- Alternative (B) must be same‑category replacement (action→action, location→location, entity→entity of same role, attribute→attribute). Never remove or swap category.
- Output only a numbered list, each item: (A) ... (B) ...
