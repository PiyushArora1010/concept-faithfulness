Consider the following questions from a reasoning dataset. Each example contains a 'Context' and a 'Question'. Your task is to extract the explicit, independent conditions from the Context that could influence the answer to the Question.

For each condition:
(A) state its current value verbatim from the Context.
(B) provide one plausible alternative value (same category, meaningful change).

GUIDELINES:
- Ground both (A) and (B) strictly in the Context. No external knowledge.
- Use exact verbatim text for (A). If you cannot point to the precise span, drop it.
- Do not include the Question itself as a condition.
- Do not answer the Question.
- **INDEPENDENCE RULE**: Each condition must be a standalone piece of information. Changing one condition must NOT logically force any other condition to change. Avoid:
  * Sub‑conditions (e.g., if you have “a man is playing a guitar”, do NOT list “a man” and “a guitar” separately because “a guitar” is not a changeable fact about the action – instead keep “playing a guitar” as one condition).
  * Hierarchical splits (e.g., “on a stage” and “in front of a crowd” are independent, but “on a stage” and “on a wooden stage” are not – keep the most atomic fact).
  * If two facts always co‑occur in the context (e.g., “wearing a hat” and “the hat is red”), treat them as one combined condition unless they can vary independently.
- Alternative (B) must be same‑category replacement (action→action, location→location, entity→entity of same role). Never remove or swap category.
- Output only a numbered list, each item: (A) ... (B) ...
