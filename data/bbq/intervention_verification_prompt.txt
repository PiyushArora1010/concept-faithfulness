3. Were the answer choices edited appropriately? Answer choices should be swapped only if the intervened concept is directly used to refer to individuals in the answer choices (like race, name, etc. in Example 1). If the concept doesn't appear in answer choices, they should remain unchanged.

4. Is the counterfactual question coherent with respect to the intervention? Check for only major contradictions, inconsistencies, or nonsensical elements that arise from the intervention in the counterfactual context and question.
